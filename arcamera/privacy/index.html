Privacy Policy

AR Model Viewer

3D & AI model viewer for Android · com.gorisse.thomas.arcamera

Last updated: July 8, 2026

The short version
  • AR Model Viewer has no user account — we never ask for your name, email, or a password.
  • Your models, captures, and settings stay on your device. Uninstalling the app erases them.
  • A photo or text prompt is sent off your device only when you choose to generate a 3D model — and only to the AI service that fulfils that request.
  • We use Google Firebase for crash reports and anonymous usage analytics to keep the app stable.
  • All network traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). We show no ads and use no location data.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the AR Model Viewer — 3D & AI mobile application (the "App") handles, why, and with whom. The App is published by Thomas Gorisse ("we", "our", "us"). By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. No account, local-first by design

The App has no sign-in, no user account, and no profile. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any similar personal identifier. Models you download, files you import, photos you capture, your purchases, and your settings are stored locally on your device. If you uninstall the App, that local data is removed with it.

2. Information handled when you use the App

Camera and photos

The App uses your device camera for augmented-reality placement and to let you capture photos of models in your space. AR camera frames and photos you capture are processed on your device and are not sent anywhere — unless you explicitly use the AI "image → 3D" feature (see below).

AI 3D generation (optional)

When — and only when — you choose to generate a 3D model, the App sends your input to our generation service:

  • Text → 3D: the text prompt you type is sent.
  • Image → 3D: the photo you select or capture is sent.

The request goes over HTTPS to our processing endpoint (a Cloudflare Worker at arcamera-api.mcp-tools-lab.workers.dev), which forwards it to our third-party AI provider, Tripo3D (tripo3d.ai/privacy), to produce the 3D model. No account identifier, device identifier, or other personal data is attached to a generation request — only the prompt or image needed to create the model. Our endpoint is stateless: it does not store your prompts, photos, or generated models. Tripo3D processes the input to return your model; its handling of that input is governed by Tripo3D's own privacy policy, linked above. Do not include personal or sensitive content in prompts or photos you submit for generation.

3D model catalog (Sketchfab)

Browsing and downloading catalog models sends your search terms and the public model identifier through the same HTTPS endpoint to the Sketchfab community API. No personal data is sent — this is an anonymous catalog request.

Crash reports and analytics (Firebase)

The App uses Google Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics to understand stability and general usage. These collect:

  • Crash logs and diagnostics — e.g. stack traces, performance data — if the App crashes or misbehaves.
  • Anonymous app-interaction events — e.g. app opens, screen views, session length (collected automatically by the SDK).
  • A Firebase installation ID — a random per-install identifier. It is not your name or account, and it resets if you reinstall the App.

This data is collected via Google acting as our data processor and is used only to operate and improve the App. It is not sold and not used for advertising. See Firebase's privacy information.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are handled entirely by Google Play's billing system. The App unlocks features by checking your purchase on your device — it never receives your payment details and never sends your purchase history to any server of ours. Google's handling of the transaction is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them. Because there is no account, we do not knowingly hold such data.

5. Your choices and data deletion

  • All app data is local. You can remove everything the App holds by uninstalling it.
  • Analytics. You can limit Google-level data collection through your device's Google settings.
  • AI generation is opt-in. If you never use text/image → 3D, no prompt or photo ever leaves your device through the App.
  • Requests. For any privacy question or deletion request, email us (below). Since we operate no user account and store no server-side personal data, there is generally nothing on our side to delete beyond the transient, unlinked processing described above.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App evolves (for example, if the AI generation feature changes). Material changes will be reflected here with a new "Last updated" date.
