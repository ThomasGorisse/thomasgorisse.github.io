Frequently Asked Questions

What percentage do I type into Bambu Studio, PrusaSlicer, Cura, or Chitubox to resize 32mm to 75mm?

Enter 234.38% uniformly across X, Y, and Z. Because volume grows with the cube of linear scale (k³), resin or filament consumption increases by roughly 12.87×, so consider hollowing resin prints with 2–2.5 mm walls and drain holes.

How do millimeter miniature scales (28mm, 32mm, 75mm) map to ratio scales (1:24, 1:35, 1:64)?

Using a standard 180 cm (1,800 mm) adult reference figure, 75mm equals exact 1:24 scale, 54mm sits near 1:33–1:35, 32mm corresponds to ~1:56, 28mm aligns with ~1:64, and 15mm sits around 1:100–1:120. Millimeter-to-millimeter conversions (e.g. 28mm to 32mm = 114.29%) and ratio-to-ratio conversions (e.g. 1:35 to 1:24 = 145.83%) are exact.

How can I verify the physical size before spending hours printing?

Check your mesh dimensions and polygon budget in our local GLB/STL/OBJ AR readiness checker, then open the same file in 3D AR Viewer on Android or iOS (powered by open-source SceneView, which has more than 1,300 stars on GitHub) to preview it at true 1:1 scale on your desk.
