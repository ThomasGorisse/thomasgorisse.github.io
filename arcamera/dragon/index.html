Placed a 3D dragon in my living room in AR — then brought it to life

When you anchor a 3D model on your real floor first with ARCore and Filament, you get exact perspective, true 1:1 scale, and matching room shadows before animating a single frame.

Get 3D AR Viewer on Google Play Get 3D AR Viewer on iOS (App Store) Inspect local .STL / .OBJ / .GLB files in browser
How it works:
1. Open any local .glb, .stl, or .obj model (or create one from a photo).
2. Anchor it on your real floor or desk at 1:1 scale with Filament PBR lighting and ground shadows.
3. Capture and animate directly in your real room.
