Mobile AR Performance Guidelines (Android ARCore & iOS ARKit)

Green · < 50,000 tris & < 15 MB

Smooth 60 fps on Android & iOS AR. Fast load times, crisp real-time lighting, and low thermal load on mid-range phones.

Amber · 50k–150k tris (or 15–30 MB)

Decimate for older phones. Runs well on recent flagships, but reducing polycount or resizing 4K textures to 2K improves stability.

Red · > 150,000 tris (or > 30 MB)

Too heavy for mobile AR — optimize mesh first. Use Blender Decimate modifier, bake normal maps, or export compressed GLB.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many triangles should a GLB, STL, or OBJ have for smooth mobile AR?

Aim for under 50,000 triangles and under 15 MB for consistent 60 fps tracking on both Android and iOS. For raw 3D scans or high-poly sculpts above 150,000 triangles, apply a Decimate modifier in Blender (target ratio 0.15–0.30) before loading on a phone.

Why does my STL or OBJ show huge or tiny measurements?

GLB uses meters by specification, while most 3D printing STL files use millimeters and OBJ files depend on the exporting software. Tap the mm, cm, m, or in unit toggle above the dimensions to match your CAD or slicer export unit immediately.

How can I test my 3D file in real augmented reality?

Once your mesh passes the readiness check, open it in 3D AR Viewer on Android or iOS — built with open-source SceneView (more than 1,300 stars on GitHub and over 500 public repositories declaring it as a dependency).
