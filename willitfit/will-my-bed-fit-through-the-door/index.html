Bed and mattress door size checker

Will my bed fit through the door?

An assembled bed often needs taking apart before it will fit through a door. A mattress is a separate check: its thickness and shorter edge must clear the opening when carried on edge, with room to line it up on both sides.

Measure the largest rigid piece you will carry, including any headboard or fittings that stay attached. Check each dismantled piece and the mattress separately. Use the door’s clear width and height inside the frame, and allow space for hands and padding.

Before you move it

Should I check the assembled bed or its separate parts?

Check what you will actually carry. Frames often come apart into side rails, slats and a headboard; measure and check every large rigid piece. App frame presets include the outer frame and headboard, so a 160 × 200 cm mattress does not mean the whole bed is that size.

Can I bend a mattress or a box spring through the door?

Some mattresses flex a little, but the permitted amount depends on their construction and the manufacturer’s instructions. Do not assume you can fold one. A box spring or rigid divan base does not bend like a mattress: measure it as a separate rigid object. The checker does not simulate flex or compression.

What if my bed fits the door but there is a hallway corner?

The doorway result only checks a straight pass. Long bed rails, a headboard, a mattress or a box spring still need room to swing around the corner. Check each piece against both hallway widths and the ceiling, and allow space to get it into the chosen orientation.
