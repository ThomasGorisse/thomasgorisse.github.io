Will a washing machine fit through the door?

A washing machine fits through a door upright when its width or depth clears the opening’s width and its height clears the opening’s height. A nominal 60 cm appliance still needs measuring: the door, controls and hose connections can stick out.

Measure the clear opening inside the frame, accounting for hinges and the open door. If needed, remove the room door and measure again. Keep the appliance upright, and leave space for hands and protective padding.

Before you move it

Do I need transit bolts to move a washing machine?

For a front-loading washer, refit the correct transit bolts before transport as instructed by the manufacturer. They secure the drum and protect its suspension. Drain residual water, disconnect and secure hoses, and remove the bolts again before running the machine. A dryer has its own transport instructions; do not assume it uses washer transit bolts.

Can I put a washing machine on its side to fit through the door?

Keep it upright and follow the manufacturer’s moving instructions. Laying it down can damage internal parts or let residual water leak. This size checker tries all six rigid-box orientations; a result on its side or back is not a recommendation to move an appliance that way.

Does fitting through the door mean it will fit in its niche?

No. Measure the niche width and height separately, and allow the depth required by the appliance’s door, rear hose connections and hose bends. Follow the installation clearances for your model, including ventilation and access. Do not squash hoses against the wall; dryers may also need space for a duct.

Moving advice: Bosch’s washing machine transport guidance. Use your own model’s manual for bolts and installation clearances.
