How is the 90° hallway corner turn calculated?

The calculator sweeps every angle from 0.1° to 89.9° around the inner corner using the exact corridor clearance formula (a·sin θ + b·cos θ − w) / (sin θ · cos θ) across all 6 ways to carry the object (upright, on its side, or on its back). It flags the tightest bottleneck: ceiling, corridor width, or corner turn.

How does elevator diagonal placement work?

First it checks the elevator door opening width and height and the cabin ceiling. If the object does not fit straight on the cabin floor, it sweeps angles from 0° to 90° to find the maximum diagonal floor clearance.
