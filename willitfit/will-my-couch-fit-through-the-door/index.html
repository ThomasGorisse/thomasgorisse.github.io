Door size checker

Will my couch fit through the door?

For a straight pass, the couch’s depth or height must fit the door’s clear width, and the other must fit its clear height. Its length travels through the doorway. Standing it on its end is another option if there’s room.

Measure the clear opening inside the frame, with the door open: stops, hinges and handles included. Measure the couch’s full width, depth and height, including feet and arms. If parts come off, measure it again without them. Leave room for hands and protective padding.

Before you move it

What should I measure on the door?

Measure the clear opening inside the frame with the door open. Include any space taken by stops, hinges and handles; use the narrowest usable width and height, not the door leaf size.

Can a couch longer than the door is tall still fit?

Yes. Its length can travel through the opening while its depth and height fit across and up, in either order. This also needs room to line it up and carry it out on the other side.

Does a no verdict mean it is impossible?

No. This checks a solid rectangular box passing straight through in six orientations. Diagonal tilting, a sofa’s shaped seat and removable parts can change the answer. Remeasure after removing parts, and allow room for hands and padding.
