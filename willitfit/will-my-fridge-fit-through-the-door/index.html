Fridge door size checker

Will my fridge fit through the door?

A fridge clears a doorway upright if its width or depth fits the clear opening’s width and its height fits the opening’s height. Measure the actual fridge, including doors and handles, and the usable gap inside the door frame.

If the opening is tight, removing the room door from its hinges may help. Fridge handles and doors may also come off: follow the model’s instructions and remeasure what you will actually carry. Keep the fridge upright; a geometric fit on its side is not moving advice.

Before you move it

Should I remove the room door or the fridge doors?

Measure the clear gap inside the frame, with stops, hinges and handles accounted for. Taking the room door off its hinges can increase usable space. Some fridge handles and doors can also be removed following the model’s instructions; water lines and wiring may need attention. Remeasure the remaining fridge body and opening rather than subtracting a guessed amount.

Why should I carry a fridge upright?

Tilting or laying a fridge down can move compressor oil out of its reservoir and into the cooling pipes. Keeping it upright helps avoid this. The checker tries all six box orientations, so a side or back size match does not mean the manufacturer permits that position.

How long should I wait before switching the fridge on?

As general guidance, leave the fridge unplugged and upright for at least four hours after moving so compressor oil can settle. The required time varies by model and how it travelled; after side transport it may be longer, sometimes up to 24 hours. Follow your own manufacturer’s manual before switching it on.

Manufacturer guidance: Beko’s four-hour settling advice and Bosch’s fridge transport guidance. Your model’s manual takes priority.
