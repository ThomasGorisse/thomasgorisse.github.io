Will It Fit

Privacy Policy

Will It Fit measures whether an object fits in your room, on your phone. The app itself collects nothing; Google's AR service collects a few technical details, described below.

Last updated: 25 November 2026 · applies to Will It Fit 3.0 and later (Android, com.gorisse.thomas.willitfit)

What the app collects

Nothing, for our part. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. It does not have the Android INTERNET permission, so it cannot send anything to us or to anyone else. We never receive any data about you.

What stays on your phone

  • The last dimensions you entered and your unit (centimetres or inches), so the app opens where you left it. They are stored in the app's private storage and removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.
  • The camera image is used live to place the object in augmented reality. The app does not record, store or send camera frames.

Sharing a check

From version 3.0.9, when you share a door, boot or hallway check, the message carries a link such as thomasgorisse.github.io/willitfit/check/#o=220x95x85&door=80x204: the object's and the space's sizes, and nothing else. You choose where it goes. The sizes sit after the #, a part of a link that browsers never send to a server: whoever opens it, in the app or on that page, reads them on their own phone. If they install the app from that page, the sizes go to Google Play inside the store link, so that the app can open the same check on its first launch.

Google services the app relies on

  • Google Play Services for AR (ARCore) provides augmented reality on supported phones. This application runs on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google and governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Whenever AR is used, Google collects, for its own analytics and to keep AR working well: your Google account identifier if you are signed in, or a device identifier if you are not; performance and diagnostic data (for example frame rate, loading time, battery); and which ARCore functions the app calls. Google says it does not share this data with third parties and encrypts it in transit. This collection is required by ARCore and is not optional. See how Google Play Services for AR handles your data. Will It Fit does not use Cloud Anchors or the Geospatial API, so no location or camera video is sent.
  • Google Play Install Referrer (from version 3.0.9): on its first launch the app asks Google Play, on the phone, which link it was installed from. When that link was a check someone shared with you, the app opens that check. The answer comes from the Play Store app on your phone; Will It Fit sends nothing.
  • Google Play In-App Review may ask whether you want to rate the app. The rating, if you give one, goes to Google Play under Google's terms; the app never sees it.

Children

The app is not directed at children. The app itself collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version of the app ever needs to collect data itself, this page will say so before that version is released, and the Play Store listing's Data safety section will be updated with it.
