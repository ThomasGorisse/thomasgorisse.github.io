Will my sofa fit through the door or 90° hallway corner?

Most moving-day surprises happen at the narrowest doorway or a right-angle apartment hallway turn — not the living room itself. Here is how movers check clearance in 60 seconds.

Check your own sizes: door checker · 90° hallway corner checker

The 4 tricks movers use when a couch looks 5–15 cm too wide

  • Unscrew the feet / legs: usually recovers 8 to 15 cm (3–6 in) of height instantly.
  • Use the diagonal depth: measure from the top back edge to the front seat frame — if that diagonal is smaller than your doorway width, the sofa hooks through around the backrest.
  • Lift the door off its hinges: recovers 3 to 4 cm (1.5 in) plus eliminates the door handle obstruction.
  • Stand it vertically around a 90° corner: if the ceiling is ≥ 240 cm and the sofa is ≤ 225 cm long, standing it on end lets it pivot in a 90 × 90 cm hallway turn.
